
# Chauka

**An interactive data story about climate change, rising sea levels, and resistance in the Pacific.**

🌐 **[Explore the story](https://chauka.vercel.app)**

🎧 *Sound on for the full experience.*

## About the project

*Chauka* combines climate data, interactive visualizations, storytelling, and sound to explore the impacts of climate change on Pacific Island communities.

The project was created in collaboration with **Bertha Ngahan**, a storyteller from Manus Island, Papua New Guinea, for the **Pacific Dataviz Challenge**.

## The story

Chauka is a bird endemic to Manus Island, deeply rooted in local culture. In many stories, it acts as a timekeeper or a messenger warning of change.

Inspired by this symbolism, the experience moves between storytelling and data visualization.

As climate disasters intensify, floods gradually overwhelm the charts and text, disrupting the viewer's ability to focus. Acts of resistance push back against this growing chaos.

Eventually, both disasters and resistance transform into stars in an inverted night sky, inviting us to reflect on our role in shaping the future for ourselves and generations to come.

## Built with

Next.js · React · Three.js · JavaScript

The experience uses interactive visualizations, climate datasets, animation, and sound.
